title: "The Signal From Ice"
date: 2026-02-02
summary: "A salvage crew intercepts a voice trapped inside a drifting glacier."
---

The ice had been adrift for two decades, a white cathedral of fracture lines and old storms. When the first signal came in, nobody believed it. We followed it anyway.

At night, the glacier hummed. Not from the wind, but from something inside it.
